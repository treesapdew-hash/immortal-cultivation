Delete Your Account

Immortal Cultivation · Last updated 20 September 2026

You can request deletion of your Immortal Cultivation account and associated personal data at any time. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account in the game

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon at the top).
  2. Tap Login & Cloud Save → Manage.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the deletion.

Deletion initiated through the in-game deletion function is processed immediately or as soon as technically necessary to complete the deletion across our systems.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the game, email cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Include either your Player ID or the email address linked to your account so we can identify the correct account.

We may ask for reasonable verification to make sure the request relates to your account. We aim to complete verified email deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm when the request has been completed.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and authentication record, including the linked email address where applicable.
  • Your cloud save, progression, settings, rankings and virtual items associated with the deleted account.
  • Your cultivator profile, sect membership, sect contributions and other account-linked gameplay records that are no longer required.
  • Other personal data associated with the account that we are not required or reasonably permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

What may remain after deletion

  • De-identified sect chat history. Messages you previously sent may remain in the sect's conversation history only after they are no longer reasonably linkable to your deleted account. They may be shown under a generic name such as "Departed Cultivator".
  • Sect continuity. If you were the sect leader, leadership may pass to another eligible member. If no members remain, the sect may be removed.
  • Limited records required for legitimate purposes. We may retain the minimum information reasonably necessary where required by law or for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or enforcement of our Terms. Any such information is kept only for as long as the relevant purpose requires.
  • Google Play records. Purchase and payment records held independently by Google Play are controlled by Google under its own policies. We do not control deletion of Google's own transaction records.

Need help?

For questions about account deletion or personal data, contact our Data Protection Officer / Privacy Contact at cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com.
