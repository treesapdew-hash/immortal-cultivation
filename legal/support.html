Support

Immortal Cultivation · Player support · Last updated 4 October 2026

Need help with gameplay, your account, cloud saves or a purchase? Email cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com.

What to include

  • Your Player ID and game version, shown in Settings.
  • Your device model and whether you are playing on Android or iOS.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that lead to the issue.
  • A screenshot or short recording if it helps explain the problem. For an event or reward issue, include the event name and approximate time with your time zone.

Do not send your password, sign-in verification codes or full payment-card details. Review screenshots and reports before sending them.

Report a bug

  1. Open Settings using the gear icon.
  2. Find Report a bug and tap Copy Report.
  3. Paste the report into an email to cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com. Add the steps to reproduce the issue and any useful screenshots.

Copy Report places diagnostic information on your clipboard. It does not send a report automatically.

Accounts and cloud saves

Open Settings → Login & Cloud Save → Manage to check the account on your device. To access a linked account on another device, use the same linked email address. If you have forgotten your password, use Forgot password? on the Log In screen.

If your progress looks wrong, keep the game installed and email us before clearing app data, creating another account or replacing a save. Include your Player ID, cultivator name, approximate stage and the last time your progress looked correct.

Restore from Cloud replaces this device's progress with the selected account's cloud save. Check the account and the progress shown before confirming a save choice. A guest account may become inaccessible if its local sign-in is lost, such as after uninstalling the game; recovery is not guaranteed.

Purchases

Purchases may be unavailable in some builds. A button labelled Coming soon, or the message “Purchases open later. Nothing was charged.”, means that build cannot complete the purchase.

When purchases are enabled, Android purchases are processed through Google Play and iOS purchases through the Apple App Store.

If the store shows a charge but the item or entitlement is missing, email us with your Player ID, the product name, store, purchase time and the order or transaction ID from the store receipt. Do not buy the item again just to try to resolve the issue.

Use the original game account when recovering a purchase. Keep the app installed while a delivery is unresolved. Purchases cannot be transferred to another game account, and deleting or resetting a character may remove currency or items that have already been delivered. An account deletion does not itself request a store refund.

If the game says your account needs purchase review, contact us with your Player ID and the relevant store receipt. Do not clear app data or repeat the purchase to bypass the review.

For billing or refund questions, see the help for the store that processed the purchase: Google Play refund policies or Apple purchase and refund help. Refund availability depends on the store's rules and the circumstances of the purchase.

Privacy and account deletion

Read our Privacy Policy for information about your data. To delete an account, follow our account deletion instructions. Account deletion permanently removes the associated game progress.
