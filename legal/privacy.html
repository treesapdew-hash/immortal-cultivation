Privacy Policy

Immortal Cultivation · Last updated 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Immortal Cultivation ("the game", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses, protects and retains information when you use the game. The game is operated from Singapore. For privacy or data-protection questions, contact our Data Protection Officer / Privacy Contact at cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication information. The game may create a Player ID or account identifier so your progress can be associated with you. If you choose to link an account, we also process your email address. If you use email/password login, authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider; we do not have access to your plaintext password.
  • Game data. Your cultivator name, stage, realm, progression, cloud save, in-game currencies and items, settings, rankings and other gameplay information.
  • Sect (guild) and community data. The sects you create or join, your role and contributions, and chat messages or other content you submit through community features. Other players may be able to see your cultivator name, progress and content where the feature is designed to be shared.
  • Technical, security and diagnostic data. Our service providers may process information such as account or Player ID, IP address, timestamps, app version, device or operating-system information, request/error information and security logs where needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the game.
  • Advertising data. If the game shows ads, Google AdMob may process information such as your IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Approximate location may be inferred from your IP address. See section 4.
  • Purchase information. If you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the payment. We may receive purchase status, product/entitlement information and transaction identifiers needed to deliver or verify the purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Support and bug reports. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, Player ID, message and attachments. If you use an in-game bug-report function that copies diagnostic information to your clipboard, nothing is sent to us until you choose to submit it.

We do not intentionally collect your real name, contacts, photos or precise location through normal gameplay.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the game, create or manage accounts, save and restore progress, enable cloud save and allow linked-account access across supported devices.
  • To operate gameplay systems such as sects, rankings, chat, rewards, purchases and other game features.
  • To keep the game fair and safe, including detecting cheating, exploits, abuse, fraud or misuse and moderating community features.
  • To show and measure rewarded ads that you choose to watch and to support operation of the game.
  • To diagnose technical problems, protect our systems and improve reliability.
  • To respond to support requests, privacy requests and legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Who we disclose information to

We do not sell your personal data. We disclose information only where reasonably necessary to operate the game, provide requested features, protect the service or comply with law. Our current service providers include:

  • Supabase: backend hosting, database and authentication services.
  • Google AdMob: rewarded advertising and related measurement, security and advertising services. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play: game distribution and processing of in-app purchases.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process or a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or violations of our Terms of Service.

4. Ads and your choices

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Depending on your region and applicable law, you may be asked to make advertising-consent choices before personalised advertising is used. You can also reset or limit use of your advertising identifier through your device settings where supported.

5. International data transfers

Some of our service providers operate systems or process information outside Singapore. Where personal data is transferred outside Singapore, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer is handled in accordance with applicable Singapore data-protection requirements, including requirements intended to provide a standard of protection comparable to the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA").

6. How long we keep information

  • Account and game data is generally kept for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the game.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify personal data associated with that account in accordance with our account deletion process, subject to limited retention where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Sect chat messages may remain in sect history only in a de-identified form that is no longer reasonably linkable to your deleted account, for example under the name "Departed Cultivator".
  • Support communications and security/diagnostic records may be kept for a limited period where reasonably necessary to resolve requests, protect the game, investigate misuse or meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted or de-identified.
  • Guest or unlinked accounts may become inaccessible if local credentials are lost, for example after uninstalling the game or changing devices, and may be removed after an extended period of inactivity.

7. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal data we hold about you, or to ask questions about how it has been used or disclosed. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis on which we rely; however, withdrawing consent may mean that we can no longer provide features that require the relevant information.

You can delete your account inside the game at Settings → Login & Cloud Save → Manage → Delete Account, or use our account deletion page if you cannot access the game. You can also contact cultivationimmortal0@gmail.com for privacy, access, correction or deletion requests.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. Additional age or parental-consent requirements may apply depending on your country or region.

9. Security and data breaches

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including encrypted network connections and access controls. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will assess and handle it in accordance with applicable law, including notifying the Personal Data Protection Commission and affected individuals where notification is legally required.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the date above. Where appropriate, significant changes may also be announced in the game.
